# About the „openESHER“ pages

„openESHER" is a free and open source project for all who are interested in how to make use of AI 
Large Language Models (LLMs) locally. 

The project is provided under GPL-3.0. You are welcome to use, study, modify and share it, 
provided you follow the GPL's term, such a spreserving license and copyright notices 
and sharing the corresponding source when distributing covered versions. 
Please contribute back when you can!
